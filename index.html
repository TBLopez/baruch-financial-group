How we work

Open to every major. Run like a real desk.

Most finance clubs are a mailing list and a guest speaker. We run training cohorts, a live portfolio with a written investment process, and a mentorship pipeline that puts members in front of alumni who hire.

No major, no dues, no gatekeeping

Roughly 40% of each cohort comes from outside the business school. Every program on this site is free, and training materials are covered by the alumni fund.

Real money, real process

$200K of an alumni-funded portfolio, managed by students. Every position needs a written thesis, a risk case and a committee vote — and the alumni board gets a report each semester.

The network does the rest

600 alumni across banking, markets, asset management and consulting. Structured mentorship, mock interviews, and a referral list built over fifteen years.
